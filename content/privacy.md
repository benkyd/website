**Effective:** 24 October 2026 · **Site:** benkyd.co.uk, run by Ben Kyd

### 1. My commitment

I will never sell, share, rent or trade information about you, to anyone, for any reason, unless compelled by a valid UK court order.

### 2. Principles

**No tracking.** No analytics, no profiling, no cookies.\
**No third parties.** Nothing on this site is loaded from anyone else's servers.\
**No collection without purpose.** Only what a web server needs to run.\
**No sharing.** Ever.

### 3. What's collected

**Server logs:** IP address, page, browser user agent, referring page and time. Kept for 14 days for security, then deleted.\
**Email:** if you write to me, your address and message, used only to reply.\
**Never collected:** cookies, analytics, location, device fingerprints, accounts.

### 4. Where it's stored

On a server I own and run at home in the UK, not with a cloud provider. Connections are HTTPS only.

### 5. Cloudflare

The one third party. Traffic passes through Cloudflare to protect my server. Cloudflare can see those requests, operates worldwide, and may set a cookie if it needs to check you're human. [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

### 6. AI training

Nothing on this site, writing or photographs, may be used to train AI. AI crawlers are blocked, and text and data mining rights are reserved under Article 4 of the EU Copyright Directive.

### 7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its processing, or take a copy. Email me with your IP address and roughly when you visited, and I'll find anything that's there. You can also complain to the [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/).

### 8. Legal requests

I'll only hand over data under a valid UK court order, and will refuse anything less. Where the law allows, I'll tell you first. Logs are deleted after 14 days, so there is rarely anything to hand over.

### 9. Changes

This policy is signed with my [PGP key](/pgp/) ([signed copy](/privacy.txt)). Any change will be dated, signed, and won't apply to data collected before it.
